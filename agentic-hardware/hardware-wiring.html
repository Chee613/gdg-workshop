Exact parts and preparation
PartQuantity / preparation
ESP32-DevKitC V4 with ESP32-WROOM-32E1, with soldered male headers; GPIO25 and the GND pin shown in the photo
Adafruit MOSFET driver #56481; REC solders leads to GND, V+ and In on the four-pad edge. Viewed from the front as pictured: GND, V+, In, Out from left to right. Leave Out unused; use the two load terminals.
5V white analog LED strip #58511m; about 1.3A at 5V. Connect one pair of leads and insulate each unused lead individually.
Regulated 5V / 2A adapter1, centre-positive 5.5mm / 2.1mm barrel plug. The reference photo shows a US plug; choose a local plug version or suitable plug adapter.
Female DC jack to screw-terminal adapter #3681; follow its moulded + and − labels
WAGO 221 three-port ground connector1 connector; ESP32 GND, supply − and MOSFET GND each occupy one port
Micro-USB data cable and wire1 USB cable; 22 AWG lamp power leads; female-to-bare jumper leads for GPIO25 and GND, with 22 AWG for the ground connector

Disconnect power while wiring. Keep the external adapter away from the ESP32 5V and 3V3 pins; USB supplies the ESP32. GPIO25 carries only the control signal. The MOSFET switches the LED strip's negative return. A breadboard is unnecessary for this version.

This is a proposed assembly layout using real manufacturer photos and drawn wires. It has not been physically assembled or tested. The current webcam demo still needs USB serial support and ESP32 firmware before this circuit responds to gestures.

Pin verification: Adafruit driver pinouts and Espressif board and power documentation. Component photography belongs to Espressif and Adafruit.
